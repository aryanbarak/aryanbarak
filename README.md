### 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a full-stack developer who ships real products, not just demos. After **20+ years in IT** — as an IT Manager and IT Officer for organizations such as the **British Embassy in Kabul** and **UNHCR** — I retrained in Germany and earned my **IHK degree as Fachinformatiker für Anwendungsentwicklung**.
